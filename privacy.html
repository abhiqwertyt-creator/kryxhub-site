Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what data KRYX Studio (the desktop app) and the KRYX Discord bot (KryxHub) use, why, and how you can control it. KRYX Studio is made by KRYX ("we"). Questions: abhiqwertyt@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your scenes, settings, stream keys and sign-ins are stored on your own computer. Stream keys and sign-in tokens are encrypted there.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Data from YouTube, Twitch and Kick is used only to show your chat and events inside KRYX Studio, and to post what you choose to post. It is not stored on our servers.

Signing in with Discord

KRYX Studio is for members of the KRYX Discord server. When you sign in with Discord, the app receives your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar, and checks your roles in the KRYX server to see whether you have access. The sign-in token stays on your computer.

KRYX Points, invites and rewards

If you use KRYX Points, the KryxHub bot stores your Discord user ID, display name, points balance, check-in streak, recent points history, rewards you redeemed, your invite code and the friends who used it, and minutes streamed or recorded (sent by the app every few minutes while you are live or recording). If you choose to report follower milestones, the app sends your follower or subscriber count. The leaderboard shows display names and points only.

Support tickets and crash reports

When you contact support from the app, your message, basic PC details (operating system, processor, graphics card, memory, app version) and, if you choose, the app's log file and a picture of your canvas are posted in a private ticket in the KRYX Discord. If KRYX Studio closes unexpectedly, it asks before sending a crash report (the error details, PC details and the log) to a staff-only channel. Stream keys are removed from logs before they are sent.

YouTube

If you choose "Sign in with YouTube", KRYX Studio uses YouTube API Services with your permission to:

  • read the live chat of your own active live stream (messages, Super Chats, Super Stickers and new members) and show it in the app and on your stream overlays;
  • send chat messages you type in the app to your own live chat;
  • upload a video clip to your channel when you press "Post to YouTube Shorts", with the title and visibility you choose;
  • read your channel's name and subscriber count.

This happens directly between your computer and Google. YouTube data is kept only in the app's memory while it runs and is not stored on, or sent to, KRYX servers, except the subscriber count if you use KRYX Points milestones. The YouTube sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer. We do not share YouTube data with anyone.

By using the YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove KRYX Studio's access at any time in the app (Settings > Stream > Sign out) or at Google security settings.

KRYX Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Twitch and Kick

If you sign in with Twitch, the app reads your channel's chat, follows, subscriptions, bits, raids and channel point redemptions, and sends chat messages you type, directly between your computer and Twitch. If you enter a Kick channel name, the app reads that channel's public chat and events. This data is not stored on our servers. You can disconnect Twitch in the app or at Twitch connections.

StreamElements and Streamlabs

If you paste a StreamElements or Streamlabs token, it is stored encrypted on your computer and used only to receive your alerts from those services.

How long we keep data

Data on your computer stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. KRYX Points data stays while you are a member of the KRYX server. Tickets and crash reports are kept as long as staff need them to help you.

Your choices

You can sign out of any account in the app, stop using KRYX Points, and ask us to delete the KRYX Points and support data we hold about you by emailing abhiqwertyt@gmail.com or opening a ticket in the KRYX Discord.

Children

KRYX Studio is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
